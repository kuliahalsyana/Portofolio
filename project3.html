NIGHT-VISION MOTION INTRUDER ALARM SYSTEM

Intelligent night-vision perimeter security system that automatically activates high-intensity intruder alarm sirens upon detecting suspicious movements around residential areas.

NIGHT-VISION MOTION INTRUDER ALARM

Night Motion Sensing • High-Decibel Alarm Siren • Auto-Arming Security Logic

🌙 NIGHT MOTION SENSING & AMBIENT DETECTION

Combines passive infrared (PIR) motion sensors and photoresistors (LDR) to automatically detect nocturnal conditions. Calibrated to capture human thermal radiation and trajectory changes across the property boundary.

🔔 INSTANT ALARM SIREN & STROBE DETERRENT

Upon detecting unauthorized boundary crossing, the microcontroller instantly triggers a high-decibel acoustic siren and synchronized strobe lights to deter intruders and alert homeowners immediately.

🛡️ ANTI-FALSE ALARM FILTERING & SECURITY LOGIC

Implements multi-sample debouncing algorithms and duration-threshold verification to eliminate false triggers caused by small domestic pets, wind gusts, or insects, ensuring flawless all-night reliability.
